Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used and protected when you use the GMSS Jikwoyi Portal.

Information We May Collect

Depending on the services you use, the portal may collect information such as your name, email address, class, phone number, student identification information, profile photograph and information submitted through the portal.

How Information Is Used

Information may be used to create and manage student accounts, provide portal services, display authorized student information, communicate school announcements and provide administrative services.

Information Protection

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to help protect information stored by the portal. However, no internet-based system can guarantee absolute security.

Sharing of Information

Personal information should only be accessed or shared for legitimate school, administrative, educational or legal purposes, and in accordance with applicable requirements.

Student Privacy

The portal is intended for school-related use. Personal information relating to students should be handled responsibly and accessed only by authorized persons.

Your Responsibilities

Users should keep their login information private and should immediately report suspected unauthorized access to the school administration.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the portal or its services change. Updated versions will be published on this page.
