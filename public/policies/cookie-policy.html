Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how cookies and similar technologies may be used by the GMSS Jikwoyi Portal.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small pieces of information stored by a website or browser. They can help websites remember information and maintain user sessions.

How We Use Cookies

The portal may use cookies or similar browser technologies to maintain login sessions, remember necessary preferences and help the portal operate correctly.

Essential Cookies

Some cookies may be necessary for account login and other essential portal functions. Disabling these cookies may prevent parts of the portal from working correctly.

Third-Party Services

If third-party services are added to the portal in the future, those services may use their own cookies or similar technologies. Their own privacy and cookie policies may apply.

Managing Cookies

Most web browsers allow users to control or delete cookies through their browser settings. Blocking essential cookies may affect portal functionality.
